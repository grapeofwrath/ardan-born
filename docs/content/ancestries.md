## Edain (Man)

Mortal Men, whose short lives are filled with courage and change. Quick to learn, and hardest to break when all seems lost.

**Impact:** Low · **Icon:** `white_banner` · **File:** `edain.json`

**Race item:** [Star of Elendil](star-of-elendil.md)

| Power | What it does | From |
|---|---|---|
| [Gift of Men](powers.md#gift-of-men) | Men are quick to learn and adapt. You gain 25% more experience from all sources. | ArdanBorn |
| [Hope Unquenched](powers.md#hope-unquenched) | When a blow would leave you below 30% health, you gain Resistance and Regeneration for 8 seconds. Once every 90 seconds. | ArdanBorn |
| [Long Reach](powers.md#long-reach) | You can reach blocks and entities further away than others can. | [Origins-Reborn](https://modrinth.com/plugin/origins-reborn) |
| [Strong Ankles](powers.md#strong-ankles) | You are able to jump higher by jumping while sprinting. | [Origins-Reborn](https://modrinth.com/plugin/origins-reborn) |
