title: "Rings of Power"
---
*Three Rings for the Elven-kings under the sky, Seven for the Dwarf-lords in their halls of stone, Nine for Mortal Men doomed to die, One for the Dark Lord on his dark throne.*

The Rings of Power give great strength, and every one of them is **cursed**: while you wear one, the Shadow grows in you. Anyone can wear them.

## Finding a ring

Rings can't be crafted. They turn up in the chests of **ancient cities**, **strongholds** and **bastion remnants**.

| Ring | Chance per chest |
|---|---|
| Ring of the Seven | 3% |
| Ring of the Nine | 3% |
| Narya, Nenya, Vilya, the One Ring | 0.5% each, and only while that ring isn't already in the world |

The Three and the One are **unique**: only one of each exists at a time. See [Unique rings](#unique-rings).

## Wearing a ring

- Hold the ring in your **off-hand** and **right-click** to put it on. Right-click again to take it off.
- A ring only works while it's on and in your off-hand. Moving it out of your off-hand takes it off.
- Right-clicking with a ring in your main hand only reminds you to use the off-hand.
- Only the ring in your off-hand counts, so you can't wear two at once.

## The rings

| Ring | Power while worn | Shadow per second |
|---|---|---|
| **Ring of the Seven** (the Dwarves) | Ores drop more, as if your tool had about two more levels of Fortune, and sometimes 1–3 gold nuggets too. Each ore you mine adds **+1 Shadow** (greed); the Naugrim feel no greed. | 0.5 |
| **Ring of the Nine** (Men) | **Deathless:** a killing blow leaves you at 1 heart instead, for **+40 Shadow**. **Wraith-world:** monsters more than 8 blocks away don't notice you. | 1.5 |
| **Narya**, the Ring of Fire | Fire Resistance; your melee hits set foes alight; you and players within 8 blocks have Strength I. | 0.25 |
| **Nenya**, the Ring of Adamant | Water Breathing, Dolphin's Grace and Resistance I; your gear loses no durability. | 0.25 |
| **Vilya**, the Ring of Air | Speed I and Slow Falling; every 5 seconds you and players within 8 blocks heal 1 heart. | 0.25 |
| **The One Ring** | Strength II and Resistance I; monsters more than 6 blocks away don't notice you; and Deathless (+40 Shadow). | 2 |

- Ores for the Seven are coal, iron, copper, gold, redstone, lapis, diamond, emerald and nether quartz ore. Silk Touch drops aren't multiplied.
- Deathless doesn't save you from the void or `/kill`.
- The wraith-world stops monsters from noticing you; one that you attack can still fight back.

## Shadow

Shadow runs from 0 to 100 and is kept on your character, so it survives logging out. Your action bar shows it while a ring is on.

- **Rising:** while a ring is on, at that ring's rate. **Hobbits** gain Shadow at half the rate, greed included.
- **Falling:** while no ring is on, 1 every 10 seconds (about 17 minutes from 100 to 0).
- **[Miruvor](evenstar.md)** lowers it by 15.

| Shadow | What happens |
|---|---|
| 25+ | Whispers in your ear |
| 50+ | Hunger, and now and then Darkness falls over you |
| 75+ | Wither I while the ring is on. When you take damage with the ring in your off-hand, there's a 20% chance it **puts itself on** |
| 100 | **The ring betrays you** (below) |

### Betrayal

At 100 Shadow the ring slips from your hand:

- It drops somewhere up to 16 blocks away.
- You get Wither II for 10 seconds, and your Shadow falls to 50.
- Two **Servants of the Enemy** (wither skeletons) come for you. If it was the One Ring, three **Ringwraiths** come instead: wither skeletons in black, riding skeleton horses.
- They hunt you for 2 minutes and then vanish. They drop nothing.
- Half their melee hits leave a [Morgul wound](morgul-wound.md).

## Those who hunt the rings

### The new moon

On a **new-moon night** in the Overworld, the Black Riders are abroad. If you carry **Narya, Nenya, Vilya or the One Ring** anywhere in your inventory, they may come looking for you. The Seven and the Nine don't draw them.

- Each minute of the night there's a **3%** chance they come for you, or **10%** while you're wearing the ring. Over a whole night that's about 1 in 4 if you only carry it, and about 2 in 3 if you wear it.
- They come at most once per new moon.
- **3 Ringwraiths** come, or **5** for the One Ring. They appear 32–48 blocks away, out of sight, and ride toward you. Once they're within 16 blocks, they attack.
- Your action bar warns you: *Black riders are abroad…*
- They leave at dawn, or after 5 minutes. If you stop carrying the ring (stash it in a chest, say), they lose your trail after 30 seconds and fade.
- Like all the Enemy's servants, they drop nothing, the wraith-world doesn't hide you from them, and half their melee hits leave a [Morgul wound](morgul-wound.md).

The moon's phase changes each day; a new moon comes every 8 days, four days after the full moon.

### The One Ring in the Nether

Carrying the **One Ring** into the Nether, worn or not, draws the Eye. *The Eye turns toward you.*

- **The burden:** you have Slowness II for as long as you're there with it. Hobbits bear it better, with Slowness I.
- **The Eye sees you:** Shadow rises 0.5 per second even while the ring isn't worn, on top of the ring's own rate if it is. Hobbits gain half, as always. The wraith-world doesn't hide you here.
- **The Nether stirs:** every 5 seconds, hostile creatures within 24 blocks turn on you: piglins and brutes (gold or no gold), ghasts, blazes, wither skeletons, hoglins, magma cubes. Zombified piglins are left alone.
- **The Nazgûl:** every 3 minutes there's a 30% chance 2 Ringwraiths are sent after you, from 24–32 blocks away. They hunt you as on a new moon, and give up if you leave the Nether or lose the ring.

The only way to be rid of it for good is [Mount Doom](#mount-doom).

## Unique rings

Narya, Nenya, Vilya and the One Ring are unique.

- A dropped unique ring never despawns, and can't be burned, blown up or destroyed, with one exception: Mount Doom.
- If something removes it anyway (`/kill`, or a plugin that clears dropped items), it's put straight back where it was.
- If a unique ring is **lost**, it can be found again. Lost means:
  - its dropped item falls into the void, or
  - no player has carried it in their inventory for **7 days**.
- Once a ring has been found again, any older copy still in the world **crumbles** the first time it's seen: when someone picks it up, opens the chest it's in, or logs in carrying it. *The ring slips away into shadow…*

### Mount Doom

Throw the **One Ring** into **lava in the Nether** and it is unmade:

- Every online player's Shadow drops to 0.
- The whole server is told.
- Whoever threw it in gets 30 levels' worth of experience.

After that, the One Ring can be found again. Fire won't unmake it, and only the One Ring can be unmade: Narya, Nenya and Vilya survive any fire.

## For server owners

- For ring tracking, item-clearing plugins and loot refills, see [Configuration](configuration.md#rings-of-power).
- `/ardanborn give` can give any ring; see [Commands](commands.md). Giving a unique ring makes that copy the current one.
- `/ardanborn ring release <ring>` sends a unique ring back into the loot pool if it's stuck somewhere nobody can reach; see [Commands](commands.md#ardanborn-ring-release).

A resource pack can give rings their own look via `custom_model_data` strings `ring_<ring>`, e.g. `ring_one`, `ring_narya`.
