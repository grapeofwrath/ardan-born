**ArdanBorn** is a Paper plugin for a Tolkien-themed Minecraft server. It adds:

- **Origins** for [Origins-Reborn](https://modrinth.com/plugin/origins-reborn): every player picks three, one from each layer, and gets all of their powers.
  1. [Ancestries](ancestries.md): the people you were born to (Edain, Noldor, Naugrim, Hobbit, …)
  2. [Classes](classes.md): your calling (Warrior, Ranger, Burglar, Loremaster, …)
  3. [Sizes](sizes.md): Normal or Small
- **Race items** made at a smithing table from each race's template. Anyone can use them; the race they're made for gets extra.
- **[Cirth Runes](cirth-runes.md)** that Smiths inscribe onto weapons and tools, the cursed **[Rings of Power](rings-of-power.md)**, and the **[Morgul Wound](morgul-wound.md)** that strong undead can inflict.
