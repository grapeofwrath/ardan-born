---
title: "Adornments"
---
Of the gifts and heirlooms of the Elder Days and of the War of the Ring not all were lost, and some lie hidden still, or are hoarded by the strong and the fell. They are worn upon the neck, the back or the belt, and serve all peoples alike.

## Finding adornments

They cannot be crafted. Wherever one is found, it may be any of the six.

| Where | Chance |
|---|---|
| Chests of woodland mansions, desert temples and jungle temples, and buried treasure | 3% each chest |
| Vaults of trial chambers, ominous or not | 3% each opening |
| Elder guardians and Wardens slain by a player | 10% |
| Piglin brutes, and evokers that belong to no raid, slain by a player | 1% |
| Wither skeletons slain by a player | 0.25% |

The [servants](rings-of-power.md#betrayal) sent by a ring leave none behind.

## Wearing adornments

You may wear one at the neck, one on the back and one as a belt. Open the adornments screen with the button in your inventory, which lies where the result of crafting would be while the crafting slots are empty, or with `/adornments`. Or right-click with an adornment in hand, and it is put on if you wear nothing there.

When you die they fall with the rest of your gear, unless the server [keeps them](configuration.md#adornments) or keeps all inventories.

The server offers a resource pack that draws the screen more fairly. If you decline it the screen is plain, and serves as well.

## The adornments

| Adornment | Worn | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **White Gem of Arwen** | Necklace | Darkness, Blindness and Weakness last half as long upon you, and the [Shadow](rings-of-power.md#shadow) that a worn ring adds each second is 25% less. |
| **Emeralds of Girion** | Necklace | Villagers' prices are 15% lower, rounded to the nearest whole; but wandering traders give no discount. Emerald ore has a 25% chance to yield 1 more emerald, though not with Silk Touch. |
| **Quiver of Legolas** | Back | Your arrows fly 10% faster, and each has a 25% chance to return to you, so that it is not spent. |
| **Sam's Pack** | Back | Food gives 50% more saturation, and cooked food has a 20% chance not to be used up. |
| **Golden Belt of Boromir** | Belt | While you are below half health you take 15% less damage. |
| **Sheath-belt of Andúril** | Belt | Swords have a 50% chance to lose no durability. |

Cooked food is cooked beef, porkchop, chicken, mutton, rabbit, cod and salmon, and baked potatoes. Against the void and `/kill` the Belt of Boromir does not avail.
