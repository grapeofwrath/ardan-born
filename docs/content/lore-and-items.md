## Undead

On this wiki, the undead are zombies and their kin, skeletons and their kin, the Wither, phantoms, zoglins, zombified piglins and undead horses.

## Ores

For the Vein rune and the Ring of the Seven, ores are coal, iron, copper, gold, redstone, lapis, diamond, emerald and nether quartz ore.
