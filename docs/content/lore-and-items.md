Things of Middle-earth that belong to no one ancestry.

| Page | What it is | Who it concerns |
|---|---|---|
| [Cirth Runes](cirth-runes.md) | Runes inscribed on weapons and tools | Anyone can craft and use them; only a [Smith](classes.md#smith) can inscribe them |
| [Rings of Power](rings-of-power.md) | Strong, cursed rings found in chests | Anyone can wear them |
| [Morgul Wound](morgul-wound.md) | A worsening wound dealt by strong undead | Anyone can be wounded |

They touch each other: those who carry a Ring of Power are hunted by servants who can deal a [Morgul wound](morgul-wound.md), and [Miruvor](evenstar.md#miruvor) eases both the wound and the [Shadow](rings-of-power.md#shadow) of the rings.

## Undead

On these pages, the **undead** are zombies and their kin, skeletons and their kin, the Wither, phantoms, zoglins, zombified piglins and undead horses.

## Ores

For the Vein rune and the Ring of the Seven, **ores** are coal, iron, copper, gold, redstone, lapis, diamond, emerald and nether quartz ore.
