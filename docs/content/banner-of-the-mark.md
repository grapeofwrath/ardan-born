A white horse upon a green field was the banner of the Mark, and the [Rohirrim](ancestries.md#rohirrim-horse-lord) followed it out of the North and into every war. From it are made the Edoras armor, the Spear of the Mark, and the Barding of the Mark.

When it is made it has the likeness of a green banner.

## Edoras armor

Any [armor](race-items.md#armor) of diamond or netherite may be joined with the Banner. You ride, in these tables, when you are upon a horse, donkey, mule, camel or llama, and your mount gallops when it goes 4 or more blocks in a second.

| Piece | Effect |
|---|---|
| Helmet | **Horsetail Crest:** while you ride, projectiles deal you 20% less damage. For the **Rohirrim** it is so with their mounts also. |
| Chestplate | **Wind in the Grass:** what you slay while riding yields 10% more experience, rounded up, and to the **Rohirrim** 20% more. |
| Leggings | **Firm in the Saddle:** while you ride, your attacks recover 15% faster, and those of the **Rohirrim** 25% faster. |
| Boots | **Trampling:** your mount at a gallop deals 2 hearts of damage to monsters that it runs into, to each once in a second. For the **Rohirrim** it deals 3 hearts. |
| Full set | **Death!:** each monster that you slay while riding adds 4% to your melee damage, up to 20%, and 28% for the **Rohirrim**. It ends when 10 seconds have passed without another. |

## Spear of the Mark

The spear is made from a [spear](race-items.md#weapons) of diamond or netherite.

| Who | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anyone | While you ride it deals 15% more damage. |
| Rohirrim | It deals 25% more instead. |

The bonuses of the Spear, of Death!, of [Charge of the Mark](powers.md#charge-of-the-mark) and of [Spear-hardy](powers.md#spear-hardy) are added together, and may not pass [60%](configuration.md#rohirrim).

## Barding of the Mark

The barding is made from horse armor of diamond or netherite, and a horse wears it as it would any other.

| Who | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anyone | The horse that wears it takes 20% less damage. |
| Rohirrim | While you ride the horse, it heals 1 heart every 5 seconds. |

## The Mearas

Of all horses the Mearas were the noblest, and they would be tamed by none save the Rohirrim. White is their coat, and their name is written in gold.

| | Meara | Shadowfax |
|---|---|---|
| Health | 20 hearts | 30 hearts |
| Speed, over the swiftest of common horses | 10% | 25% |
| Jump, over the highest of common horses | None | 10% stronger |
| Size | That of a horse | 1.15 times a horse |

- Each wild horse of the plains and sunflower plains has a [2%](configuration.md#mearas) chance to be one of them, and so has each foal bred by one who has [Breeder of the Mark](powers.md#breeder-of-the-mark). The foals of Mearas have no better chance than others.
- Once a Meara is tame, anyone may ride it.

### Shadowfax

Chief of the Mearas was Shadowfax, and a Meara has a 20% chance to be he. There is but one in the world, and while he lives no other may appear; yet a wild Shadowfax is not counted until he is tamed, and when one is claimed any other that is wild becomes a common Meara.

- If he is bred, he belongs to his breeder from birth.
- His owner may sneak and swap hands (F by default), and he comes from as far as 128 blocks in the same world, though not while anyone rides him or before he is grown. He may be called again after 10 seconds.
