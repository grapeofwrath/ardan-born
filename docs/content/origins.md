---
title: "Origins"
---
An **origin** is a choice about who your character is. Every player picks three, one from each layer, and has the powers of all three at once.

## The layers

They are chosen in this order:

| Layer | What it is |
|---|---|
| 1. [Ancestry](ancestries.md) | The people you were born to. Each has a [race item](race-items.md) made to suit it. |
| 2. [Class](classes.md) | The calling you follow. Any class goes with any ancestry. |
| 3. [Size](sizes.md) | How tall you stand. Defaults to Normal Size. |

## Reading an origin

- **Impact** is Origins-Reborn's rating, from None to High, of how much the origin changes the game for you.
- **Icon** is the item that stands for it.
- **File** is its file in `plugins/ArdanBorn/origins/`, for server owners.
- **From** tells you which plugin a power comes from.

## Powers

The [Powers](powers.md) page has the details of every power and which origins have it. Some come from ArdanBorn, the rest from Origins-Reborn and its addons. If an addon isn't installed, its powers do nothing; see [Optional addons](installation.md#optional-addons).

To keep an origin from being picked, see [Hiding origins](installation.md#hiding-origins).
