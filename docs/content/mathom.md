*Too good to throw away, too odd to use; until now.* The Hobbits' template, made to suit the [Hobbit](ancestries.md#hobbit-halfling). It makes the **Mithril armor**.

Like every race item, anyone can use it; Hobbits get extra.

## Crafting the Mathom

Shaped recipe in a crafting table. It also shows in the recipe book.

| | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Pumpkin Pie | Iron Ingot | Pumpkin Pie |
| Gold Ingot | Diamond | Gold Ingot |
| Pumpkin Pie | Iron Ingot | Pumpkin Pie |

The Mathom looks like a bundle. It can't be used in any other recipe.

## Mithril armor

At a **smithing table**, put the Mathom with a **diamond or netherite** armor piece (and nothing in the third slot). Pieces work like the [Arkenstone armor](arkenstone.md#armor): they keep enchantments, trim, damage and name, and can be upgraded to netherite.

| Piece | Effect |
|---|---|
| Helmet | Not designed yet |
| Chestplate | **Mithril Coat.** Anyone: any single hit that would deal more than 4 hearts (after armor) is reduced by 30%. **Hobbits:** reduced by 40%, and that hit doesn't knock you back. |
| Leggings | Not designed yet |
| Boots | Not designed yet |
| Full set | Not designed yet |

### The Mithril Coat

- It's **chain mail** with full diamond or netherite chestplate stats: light and silver, as mithril should look.
- Repair it with another Mithril chestplate of the same tier at an anvil. Upgrade diamond to netherite as usual.
- Any kind of damage counts (mobs, players, falls, explosions) except the void, `/kill` and starving. Small hits aren't changed.

A resource pack can give these items their own look via `custom_model_data` strings `mathom` and `mithril`.
