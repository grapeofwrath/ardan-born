*Too good to throw away, too odd to use; until now.* The Hobbits' template, made to suit the [Hobbit](ancestries.md#hobbit-halfling). It makes the **Mithril armor**, **Sting** and the **Longbottom Pipe**.

Like every [race item](race-items.md), anyone can use these; Hobbits get extra.

## Crafting the Mathom

Shaped recipe in a crafting table. See [Templates](race-items.md#templates).

| | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Pumpkin Pie | Iron Ingot | Pumpkin Pie |
| Gold Ingot | Diamond | Gold Ingot |
| Pumpkin Pie | Iron Ingot | Pumpkin Pie |

The Mathom looks like a bundle. It can't be used in any other recipe.

## Mithril armor

Made at a smithing table from the Mathom and any **diamond or netherite** armor piece. See [Armor](race-items.md#armor) for making, repairing and upgrading it.

| Piece | Effect |
|---|---|
| Helmet | **Merry Heart:** eating gives Regeneration I for 4 seconds. **Hobbits:** 6 seconds, shared with players within 6 blocks. |
| Chestplate | **Mithril Coat.** Anyone: any single hit that would deal more than 4 hearts (after armor) is reduced by 30%. **Hobbits:** reduced by 40%, and that hit doesn't knock you back. |
| Leggings | **Stone's Throw:** snowballs and eggs you throw deal 1 heart to monsters and make them target you. **Hobbits:** 2 hearts. |
| Boots | **There and Back:** sprinting uses 20% less hunger. **Hobbits:** 40%. |
| Full set | **Fellowship:** you and players within 8 blocks take 10% less damage. Two wearers don't add up. **Hobbits:** 12%. |

### The Mithril Coat

- It's **chain mail** with full diamond or netherite chestplate stats: light and silver, as mithril should look.
- Repair it with another Mithril chestplate of the same tier at an anvil. Upgrade diamond to netherite as usual.
- Any kind of damage counts (mobs, players, falls, explosions) except the void, `/kill` and starving. Small hits aren't changed.

## Sting

Made at a smithing table from the Mathom and a **diamond or netherite sword**. See [Weapons](race-items.md#weapons).

| Who | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anyone | **+25% damage to spiders** and cave spiders. |
| Hobbits | +25% damage to a mob that isn't targeting you. |

## Longbottom Pipe

Made at a smithing table from the Mathom and a **stick**. See [Tools](race-items.md#tools).

| Who | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anyone | You and players within 6 blocks get Regeneration I for 10 seconds and lose 5 [Shadow](rings-of-power.md#shadow). 5-minute cooldown. |
| Hobbits | 10 Shadow. |

A resource pack can give these items their own look via `custom_model_data` strings `mathom`, `mithril`, `sting` and `longbottom_pipe`.
