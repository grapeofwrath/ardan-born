Of the things that the [Hobbits](ancestries.md#hobbit) kept and could not bring themselves to part with, the Mathom was the chief; and from it are made the Mithril armor, the blade Sting, and the Longbottom Pipe.

## The making of the Mathom

| | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Pumpkin Pie | Iron Ingot | Pumpkin Pie |
| Gold Ingot | Diamond | Gold Ingot |
| Pumpkin Pie | Iron Ingot | Pumpkin Pie |

When it is made it has the likeness of a bundle.

## Mithril armor

Any [armor](race-items.md#armor) of diamond or netherite may be joined with the Mathom, and becomes thereafter a piece of this harness.

| Piece | Effect |
|---|---|
| Helmet | **Merry Heart:** whoever eats while wearing it is given Regeneration I for 4 seconds. To **Hobbits** it is given for 6 seconds, and to all players within 6 blocks of them. |
| Chestplate | **Mithril Coat:** any hit that would deal more than 4 hearts, after armor, is lessened by 30%. For **Hobbits** it is lessened by 40%, and that hit does not throw them back. |
| Leggings | **Stone's Throw:** the snowballs and eggs you cast deal 1 heart to monsters, and turn their wrath upon you. From the hands of **Hobbits** they deal 2 hearts. |
| Boots | **There and Back:** sprinting wearies you less, and takes 20% less hunger, or 40% less from **Hobbits**. |
| Full set | **Fellowship:** you and all players within 8 blocks take 10% less damage, or 12% less if the wearer is a **Hobbit**; but two who wear it together do not add to one another. |

### The Mithril Coat

The coat has the form of chain mail, yet in strength it is the equal of the diamond or netherite from which it was made. It may be mended at an anvil with another Mithril chestplate of the same tier. Against every hurt it avails, save the void, `/kill` and starvation.

## Sting

Sting is made from a [sword](race-items.md#weapons) of diamond or netherite.

| Who | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anyone | To spiders and cave spiders it is most bitter, and deals them 25% more damage. |
| Hobbits | It deals 25% more damage also to any mob that is not targeting them. |

## Longbottom Pipe

The pipe is made from a [stick](race-items.md#tools), and it is not consumed in the smoking.

| Who | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anyone | You and all players within 6 blocks receive Regeneration I for 10 seconds, and 5 [Shadow](rings-of-power.md#shadow) is lifted from each. It may not be used again until 5 minutes have passed. |
| Hobbits | 10 Shadow is lifted. |
