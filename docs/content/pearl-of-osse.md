title: "Pearl of Ossë"
---
*A pearl of the shores of Alqualondë, gift of Ossë.* The Teleri's template, made to suit the [Teleri](ancestries.md#teleri-sea-elf). It makes the **Alqualondë armor**, the **Spear of the Havens** and the **Net of the Havens**.

Like every [race item](race-items.md), anyone can use these; Teleri get extra.

## Crafting the Pearl of Ossë

Shaped recipe in a crafting table. See [Templates](race-items.md#templates).

| | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Prismarine Shard | Nautilus Shell | Prismarine Shard |
| Gold Ingot | Diamond | Gold Ingot |
| Prismarine Shard | Nautilus Shell | Prismarine Shard |

The Pearl of Ossë looks like a Heart of the Sea. It can't be used in any other recipe.

## Alqualondë armor

Made at a smithing table from the Pearl of Ossë and any **diamond or netherite** armor piece. See [Armor](race-items.md#armor) for making, repairing and upgrading it.

| Piece | Effect |
|---|---|
| Helmet | **Friend of Ossë:** drowned, guardians, elder guardians and pufferfish leave you alone. Strike one and the sea is hostile to you for 30 seconds. That includes the elder guardian's Mining Fatigue and pufferfish stings. **Teleri:** Dolphin's Grace (swift swimming) while in water. |
| Chestplate | **Swan-mail:** 12% less damage while in water or rain. **Teleri:** heal half a heart every 5 seconds there. |
| Leggings | **Undertow:** 15% chance that a mob that strikes you is swept back and slowed for 3 seconds. **Teleri:** 25%. |
| Boots | **Wave-walker:** sprint across water for up to 5 seconds at a time, renewed on land. Stop sprinting and you sink. **Teleri:** 10 seconds. |
| Full set | **Song of the Sea:** melee and trident hits in water or rain have a 15% chance to heal you and players within 6 blocks by 1 heart (at most every 3 seconds). **Teleri:** 25%. |

### The helmet

The Alqualondë helmet is a **turtle shell with the full diamond or netherite stats**. It looks like a turtle shell, and like one it gives 10 seconds of Water Breathing after you surface.

- Repair it with diamonds or netherite ingots, or another Alqualondë helmet of the same tier. Plain turtle shells don't work.
- Upgrade diamond to netherite at a smithing table with a netherite upgrade template and a netherite ingot, as usual.

## Spear of the Havens

Made at a smithing table from the Pearl of Ossë and a **trident**. See [Weapons](race-items.md#weapons).

| Who | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anyone | **Always returns:** a thrown spear flies back to you like Loyalty I, even without the enchantment. A Loyalty enchantment still works as usual. |
| Anyone | **Call of the sea:** with **Channeling**, a thrown spear calls lightning on any foe it hits that's standing in water or rain, not only in thunderstorms. In a thunderstorm, vanilla Channeling applies. |
| Teleri | **+15% damage**, thrown or swung. This stacks with the Teleri's [Tide's Fury](powers.md#tides-fury). |

## Net of the Havens

Made at a smithing table from the Pearl of Ossë and a **lead**. See [Tools](race-items.md#tools).

| Who | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anyone | Monsters within 4 blocks of where you look (up to 16 away) get Slowness IV for 4 seconds. 45-second cooldown. |
| Teleri | Netted monsters also take half a heart each second. |

A resource pack can give these items their own look via `custom_model_data` strings `pearl_of_osse`, `spear_of_the_havens`, `alqualonde` and `net_of_the_havens`.
