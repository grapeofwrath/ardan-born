title: "Pearl of Ossë"
---
On the shores of Alqualondë the [Teleri](ancestries.md#teleri-sea-elf) gathered pearls, and Ossë was their friend. From the pearl that bears his name are made the Alqualondë armor, the Spear of the Havens and the Net of the Havens.

## The making of the Pearl

| | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Prismarine Shard | Nautilus Shell | Prismarine Shard |
| Gold Ingot | Diamond | Gold Ingot |
| Prismarine Shard | Nautilus Shell | Prismarine Shard |

When it is made it has the likeness of a heart of the sea.

## Alqualondë armor

Any [armor](race-items.md#armor) of diamond or netherite may be joined with the Pearl.

| Piece | Effect |
|---|---|
| Helmet | **Friend of Ossë:** drowned, guardians, elder guardians and pufferfish do you no harm, whether by Mining Fatigue or by sting; but if you strike one, they are your enemies for 30 seconds. The **Teleri** have Dolphin's Grace in water. |
| Chestplate | **Swan-mail:** in water or rain you take 12% less damage, and there the **Teleri** heal half a heart every 5 seconds. |
| Leggings | **Undertow:** there is a 15% chance that a mob that strikes you is swept back, and slowed for 3 seconds. For the **Teleri** it is 25%. |
| Boots | **Wave-walker:** you may sprint across water for 5 seconds, and this is renewed on land; but if you cease to sprint you sink. The **Teleri** may sprint for 10 seconds. |
| Full set | **Song of the Sea:** in water or rain your melee and trident hits have a 15% chance to heal you and all players within 6 blocks by 1 heart, though no more than once in 3 seconds. For the **Teleri** it is 25%. |

### The helmet

The helmet has the form of a turtle shell, yet it has the full stats of the diamond or netherite from which it was made, and for 10 seconds after you surface it gives you Water Breathing. It may be mended with diamonds or netherite ingots, or with an Alqualondë helmet of the same tier, but not with turtle shells.

## Spear of the Havens

The spear is made from a [trident](race-items.md#weapons).

| Who | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anyone | **Always returns:** when thrown it comes back to you as with Loyalty I. A Loyalty enchantment works upon it as usual. |
| Anyone | **Call of the sea:** with Channeling, a thrown spear brings lightning upon any foe it hits in water or rain. In a thunderstorm, vanilla Channeling applies. |
| Teleri | Thrown or swung, it deals 15% more damage, and this stacks with [Tide's Fury](powers.md#tides-fury). |

## Net of the Havens

The net is made from a [lead](race-items.md#tools).

| Who | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anyone | Look to a place as far as 16 blocks away, and the monsters within 4 blocks of it have Slowness IV for 4 seconds. It may not be cast again until 45 seconds have passed. |
| Teleri | Those that are netted take half a heart of damage each second besides. |
