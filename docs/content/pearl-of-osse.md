*A pearl of the shores of Alqualondë, gift of Ossë.* The Teleri's template, made to suit the [Teleri](ancestries.md#teleri-sea-elf). It makes the **Spear of the Havens** and the **Alqualondë armor**.

Like every race item, anyone can use these; Teleri get extra.

## Crafting the Pearl of Ossë

Shaped recipe in a crafting table. It also shows in the recipe book.

| | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Prismarine Shard | Nautilus Shell | Prismarine Shard |
| Gold Ingot | Diamond | Gold Ingot |
| Prismarine Shard | Nautilus Shell | Prismarine Shard |

The Pearl of Ossë looks like a Heart of the Sea. It can't be used in any other recipe.

## Spear of the Havens

At a **smithing table**, put the Pearl of Ossë with a **trident** (and nothing in the third slot).

- It keeps its enchantments, damage and any custom name.
- It's still a normal trident, so it can be enchanted and repaired at an anvil.
- Applying the Pearl to a spear that's already upgraded does nothing.
- The spear can't be used in crafting recipes, because those would strip its upgrade.

| Who | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anyone | **Always returns:** a thrown spear flies back to you like Loyalty I, even without the enchantment. A Loyalty enchantment still works as usual. |
| Anyone | **Call of the sea:** with **Channeling**, a thrown spear calls lightning on any foe it hits that's standing in water or rain, not only in thunderstorms. In a thunderstorm, vanilla Channeling applies. |
| Teleri | **+15% damage**, thrown or swung. This stacks with the Teleri's [Tide's Fury](powers.md#tides-fury). |

## Alqualondë armor

At a **smithing table**, put the Pearl of Ossë with a **diamond or netherite** armor piece (and nothing in the third slot). Pieces work like the [Arkenstone armor](arkenstone.md#armor): they keep enchantments, trim, damage and name, and can be upgraded to netherite.

| Piece | Effect |
|---|---|
| Helmet | **Friend of Ossë:** drowned, guardians, elder guardians and pufferfish leave you alone. Strike one and the sea is hostile to you for 30 seconds. That includes the elder guardian's Mining Fatigue and pufferfish stings. **Teleri:** Dolphin's Grace (swift swimming) while in water. |
| Chestplate | Not designed yet |
| Leggings | Not designed yet |
| Boots | Not designed yet |
| Full set | Not designed yet |

### The helmet

The Alqualondë helmet is a **turtle shell with the full diamond or netherite stats**. It looks like a turtle shell, and like one it gives 10 seconds of Water Breathing after you surface.

- Repair it with diamonds or netherite ingots, or another Alqualondë helmet of the same tier. Plain turtle shells don't work.
- Upgrade diamond to netherite at a smithing table with a netherite upgrade template and a netherite ingot, as usual.

A resource pack can give these items their own look via `custom_model_data` strings `pearl_of_osse`, `spear_of_the_havens` and `alqualonde`.
