title: "Mallorn Leaf"
---
Golden were the leaves of the mallorn trees in Lothlórien, where many of the [Silvan Elves](ancestries.md#silvan) dwelt. From such a leaf are made the Lórien armor, the Bow of the Galadhrim, and the rope Hithlain.

## The making of the Leaf

| | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Any Leaves | String | Any Leaves |
| Gold Ingot | Diamond | Gold Ingot |
| Any Leaves | String | Any Leaves |

The leaves may be of any kind, and of several kinds together. When it is made it has the likeness of a lily pad.

## Lórien armor

Any [armor](race-items.md#armor) of diamond or netherite may be joined with the Leaf.

| Piece | Effect |
|---|---|
| Helmet | **Far-sighted:** arrows that hit from 20 or more blocks away deal 10% more damage, and those of the **Silvan Elves** 20% more. |
| Chestplate | **Elusive:** there is a 10% chance that the melee hit or projectile of a mob misses you, and 15% in [woodland](powers.md#woodland-stride). If you are of the **Silvan Elves**, the attacker is tangled with Slowness II for 3 seconds. |
| Leggings | **Leaf-light:** after an arrow hit you have Speed I for 3 seconds. In woodland the **Silvan Elves** have Speed II. |
| Boots | **Bough-runner:** you step up full blocks without jumping. In woodland the **Silvan Elves** have Jump Boost I. |
| Full set | **Galadhrim Volley:** an arrow hit has a 15% chance to strike a second monster within 8 blocks for half the damage. For the **Silvan Elves** it is 25%. |

### The Cloak of Lórien

The chestplate has the form of a leather tunic, yet it has the full stats of the diamond or netherite from which it was made. Its color is grey-green, and darker in netherite, and it may be dyed anew. It may be mended at an anvil with another Lórien chestplate of the same tier. Only the melee hits and projectiles of mobs can miss the wearer; the attacks of players, and falls, and fire do not.

## Bow of the Galadhrim

The bow is made from a [bow](race-items.md#weapons).

| Who | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anyone | Arrows shot at full draw deal 20% more damage to foes that are unhurt. |
| Silvan | Such a foe has also Slowness II for 3 seconds. |

## Hithlain

The rope is made from a [lead](race-items.md#tools).

| Who | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anyone | Right-click a block within 16 blocks and you are drawn to it, and your next fall within 4 seconds does you no harm. It may not be used again until 10 seconds have passed. |
| Silvan | It reaches 24 blocks, and may be used again after 6 seconds. |
