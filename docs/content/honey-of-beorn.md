---
title: "Honey of Beorn"
---
*From the hives of Beorn's hall.* The Beornings' template, made to suit the [Beorning](ancestries.md#beorning-skin-changer). It makes the **Carrock armor**, the **Hunting Bow** and the **Skin-changer's Claw**.

Like every [race item](race-items.md), anyone can use these; Beornings get extra.

## Crafting the Honey of Beorn

Shaped recipe in a crafting table. See [Templates](race-items.md#templates).

| | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Honeycomb | Honey Block | Honeycomb |
| Gold Ingot | Diamond | Gold Ingot |
| Honeycomb | Honey Block | Honeycomb |

The Honey of Beorn looks like glinting honeycomb. It can't be used in any other recipe.

## Carrock armor

Made at a smithing table from the Honey of Beorn and any **diamond or netherite** armor piece. See [Armor](race-items.md#armor) for making, repairing and upgrading it.

| Piece | Effect |
|---|---|
| Helmet | **Honey-fed:** drinking a honey bottle heals 2 hearts, at most every 10 seconds. **Beornings:** 3 hearts. |
| Chestplate | **Beeswax:** 10% chance that a mob that strikes you is poisoned for 4 seconds. **Beornings:** 15%. |
| Leggings | **Great Bounds:** jump 20% higher. **Beornings:** 35% at night. |
| Boots | **Wild Ways:** no harm from berry bushes, and 5% faster on grass. **Beornings:** 10% at night. |
| Full set | **Maul:** every 4th melee hit also strikes monsters within 3 blocks for half the damage. **Beornings:** every 3rd at night. |

## Hunting Bow

Made at a smithing table from the Honey of Beorn and a **bow**. See [Weapons](race-items.md#weapons).

| Who | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anyone | **Arrows fly true:** arrows shot from it fly dead straight, with no drop, for 3 seconds (about 150 blocks at full draw). After that, gravity takes them as usual. Aim exactly where you want to hit, even at long range. |
| Beornings | **+20% arrow damage at night** (the same night as their [Moonbound](powers.md#moonbound) power, in the Overworld). |

- Straight flight works with every arrow type, including tipped and spectral arrows, and with Power, Punch, Flame and Infinity.
- An arrow stays marked as a Hunting Bow arrow after it's fired, so the Beorning bonus applies even if you switch weapons before it lands.

## Skin-changer's Claw

Made at a smithing table from the Honey of Beorn and a **flint**. See [Tools](race-items.md#tools).

| Who | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anyone | +20% melee damage and 10% more speed for 8 seconds. 90-second cooldown. |
| Beornings | 12 seconds at night. |

A resource pack can give these items their own look via `custom_model_data` strings `honey_of_beorn`, `hunting_bow`, `carrock` and `skin_changers_claw`.
