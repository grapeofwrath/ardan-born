title: "Honey of Beorn"
---
About the hall of Beorn there were great hives, and the [Beornings](ancestries.md#beorning-skin-changer) who came after kept them still. From their honey are made the Carrock armor, the Hunting Bow and the Skin-changer's Claw.

## The making of the Honey

| | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Honeycomb | Honey Block | Honeycomb |
| Gold Ingot | Diamond | Gold Ingot |
| Honeycomb | Honey Block | Honeycomb |

When it is made it has the likeness of honeycomb that glints.

## Carrock armor

Any [armor](race-items.md#armor) of diamond or netherite may be joined with the Honey.

| Piece | Effect |
|---|---|
| Helmet | **Honey-fed:** to drink a honey bottle heals you 2 hearts, though no more than once in 10 seconds. The **Beornings** are healed 3 hearts. |
| Chestplate | **Beeswax:** there is a 10% chance that a mob that strikes you is poisoned for 4 seconds. For the **Beornings** it is 15%. |
| Leggings | **Great Bounds:** you jump 20% higher, and the **Beornings** 35% higher at night. |
| Boots | **Wild Ways:** berry bushes do you no harm, and you go 5% faster on grass. The **Beornings** go 10% faster at night. |
| Full set | **Maul:** every 4th melee hit strikes also the monsters within 3 blocks, for half the damage. For the **Beornings** at night it is every 3rd. |

## Hunting Bow

The bow is made from a [bow](race-items.md#weapons).

| Who | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anyone | **Arrows fly true:** for 3 seconds its arrows fly without falling, which at full draw is about 150 blocks. |
| Beornings | At night in the Overworld, as with [Moonbound](powers.md#moonbound), its arrows deal 20% more damage. |

- So it is with arrows of every type, and with Power, Punch, Flame and Infinity.
- The bonus of the Beornings remains upon the arrow, even if you change weapons before it lands.

## Skin-changer's Claw

The claw is made from a [flint](race-items.md#tools).

| Who | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anyone | For 8 seconds you deal 20% more melee damage and go 10% faster. It may not be used again until 90 seconds have passed. |
| Beornings | At night it lasts for 12 seconds. |
