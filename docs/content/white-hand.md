*The sign of Saruman, daubed on Uruk shields.* The Uruk-hai's template, made to suit the [Uruk-hai](ancestries.md#uruk-hai-orc). It makes the **Scimitar of Isengard**.

Like every race item, anyone can use it; the Uruk-hai get extra.

## Crafting the White Hand

Shaped recipe in a crafting table. It also shows in the recipe book.

| | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Block of Coal | White Dye | Block of Coal |
| Gold Ingot | Diamond | Gold Ingot |
| Block of Coal | White Dye | Block of Coal |

The White Hand looks like white dye. It can't be used in any other recipe.

## Scimitar of Isengard

At a **smithing table**, put the White Hand with a **diamond or netherite sword** (and nothing in the third slot). It keeps its enchantments, damage and name, and can still be upgraded to netherite as usual.

| Who | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anyone | **Finishing blows:** +25% melee damage against foes below half health. |
| Uruk-hai | During [Battle-fury](powers.md#battle-fury) (the 8 seconds after a kill), each hit heals you half a heart. |

- Sweeping-edge hits count as melee hits.
- Cirth runes can be inscribed on it like any sword; see [Cirth Runes](cirth-runes.md).

A resource pack can give these items their own look via `custom_model_data` strings `white_hand` and `scimitar_of_isengard`.
