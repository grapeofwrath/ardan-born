## ArdanBorn powers

### Axe of Durin

Axes deal 20% more damage and give Slowness I for 2 seconds.

`ardanborn:axe_of_durin` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Dwarf](ancestries.md#dwarf)

### Backstab

Melee hits from behind deal 35% more damage, or 50% more to a mob that isn't targeting you.

- "Behind" means facing the same way as the target, within about 60°.
- A mob chasing someone else counts as not targeting you.
- Critical-hit particles show when it lands.

`ardanborn:backstab` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Burglar](classes.md#burglar)

### Bane of Mirkwood

Arrows and thrown tridents deal 20% more damage while you're in a [woodland biome](powers.md#woodland-stride), and 50% more to spiders and cave spiders.

- The two bonuses stack: ×1.8 against a spider in woodland.

`ardanborn:bane_of_mirkwood` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Silvan](ancestries.md#silvan)

### Bane of the Dunlendings

Melee hits and projectiles deal 20% more damage to illagers.

- Illagers: pillagers, vindicators, evokers and illusioners. Not ravagers, vexes or witches.
- Not part of the 60% cap; multiplies with it.

`ardanborn:bane_of_the_dunlendings` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Rohirrim](ancestries.md#rohirrim)

### Battle-fury

A kill gives Speed I for 8 seconds and Strength I for 5. Killing players counts. New kills refresh the timers.

`ardanborn:battle_fury` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Uruk-hai](ancestries.md#uruk-hai)

### Breath of the Sea

Breathe underwater indefinitely.

`ardanborn:breath_of_the_sea` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Teleri](ancestries.md#teleri)

### Breeder of the Mark

Foals you breed have speed, jump and health no lower than the average of their parents, and grow up in 10 minutes. They can be one of the [Mearas](banner-of-the-mark.md#the-mearas).

- Each quality is the better of the foal's own and its parents' average.
- Works on mules, but only horses can be Mearas.

`ardanborn:breeder_of_the_mark` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Rohirrim](ancestries.md#rohirrim)

### Call of Huan

Right-click with a bone, in the air or at a block, to call Huan for 60 seconds. 3-minute cooldown, from when you call him. The bone isn't used up.

- A tamed wolf, 1.5× size: 30 hearts, 4 hearts of bite damage, +20% speed, 50% knockback resistance.
- Leaves early if you log out, die or call him again. Not saved with the world.
- Can't be fed, bred or dyed. Right-click with an empty hand to sit or stand. Drops nothing.
- Bones still tame ordinary wolves. The cooldown shows on your action bar.

`ardanborn:call_of_huan` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Beastfriend](classes.md#beastfriend)

### Charge of the Mark

Melee hits while riding deal 20% more damage. After 3 seconds of galloping, the next melee hit also knocks a monster back.

- Riding means a horse, donkey, mule, camel or llama. Galloping is 4 blocks per second or more.
- A hit that knocks a monster back starts the 3 seconds again. Bosses aren't knocked back.
- Adds to Spear-hardy, the [Spear of the Mark](banner-of-the-mark.md#spear-of-the-mark) and Death!, up to [60%](configuration.md#rohirrim) together.

`ardanborn:charge_of_the_mark` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Rohirrim](ancestries.md#rohirrim)

### Cleave

Sword and axe hits also deal half damage to one hostile mob within 2.5 blocks of the target. Never hits players, pets or villagers.

`ardanborn:cleave` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Warrior](classes.md#warrior)

### Deep Lore

Enchanting costs 1 fewer level, but never less than the option's slot number (1, 2 or 3). Lapis cost and enchantments don't change.

`ardanborn:deep_lore` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Noldor](ancestries.md#noldor)

### Delver's Instinct

Haste I and Night Vision below Y 50 where sky light is under 4.

- Night Vision lasts 16 seconds after you leave.

`ardanborn:delvers_instinct` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Dwarf](ancestries.md#dwarf)

### Elven-wrought

Tools, weapons, armor, shields, elytra and fishing rods have a 40% chance to lose no durability when used. Stacks with Unbreaking.

`ardanborn:elven_wrought` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Noldor](ancestries.md#noldor)

### First Strike

A melee hit after 3 seconds without one deals 50% more damage. Every melee hit that lands restarts the timer.

`ardanborn:first_strike` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Warrior](classes.md#warrior)

### Forge-hardened

40% less damage from fire, burning, lava and magma blocks. You can still catch fire. Works alongside Fire Resistance.

`ardanborn:forge_hardened` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Dwarf](ancestries.md#dwarf)

### Fortunate Folk

Permanent Luck I: better fishing treasure and some chest loot.

`ardanborn:fortunate_folk` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Hobbit](ancestries.md#hobbit)

### Friend of Beasts

Wild beasts don't target you unless you strike first. Your tamed animals take half damage, and heal 1 heart every 10 seconds within 16 blocks of you.

- Beasts: wolves, bees, polar bears, llamas, trader llamas, pandas, goats.
- Half damage covers every animal you've tamed, from any source, at any distance, while you're online. Not void or `/kill`.
- [Huan](#call-of-huan) counts.

`ardanborn:friend_of_beasts` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Beastfriend](classes.md#beastfriend)

### Gift of Men

25% more experience from all sources, rounded up per orb or reward (a 1-point orb gives 2).

- Sources include mobs, ore, smelting, breeding, fishing and trading.

`ardanborn:gift_of_men` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Dúnedain](ancestries.md#dúnedain)

### Green Thumb

Each second, crops within 5 blocks (2 up or down) have a 5% chance to grow a stage, and saplings and mangrove propagules a 1% chance to grow as if bone-mealed.

- Crops: wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroots, nether wart, sweet berries, cocoa, torchflowers, melon and pumpkin stems. Not pitcher plants.
- Wheat takes about 2-3 minutes from seed to ripe.

`ardanborn:green_thumb` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Herbalist](classes.md#herbalist)

### Hardened Veteran

+2 hearts and +25% knockback resistance, which stacks with netherite armor. The new hearts start empty.

`ardanborn:hardened_veteran` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Warrior](classes.md#warrior)

### Healer of Imladris

Instant Health, Regeneration and golden apples heal you 50% more.

- Any source: beacons, [Star of Eärendil](#star-of-eärendil), [Staff of Rest](#staff-of-rest), [Healing Presence](#healing-presence).
- Not natural regeneration from hunger.

`ardanborn:healer_of_imladris` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Peredhil](ancestries.md#peredhil)

### Healing Presence

Every 10 seconds, you and players within 6 blocks heal 1 heart. Players at full health are skipped. Several Healers stack.

- [Healer of Imladris](#healer-of-imladris) raises it to 1.5 hearts for a Peredhil.

`ardanborn:healing_presence` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Healer](classes.md#healer)

### Hope Unquenched

Damage that leaves you alive at or below 30% health gives Resistance I and Regeneration I for 8 seconds. 90-second cooldown, from when it triggers.

`ardanborn:hope_unquenched` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Dúnedain](ancestries.md#dúnedain)

### Hunt Prey

The last mob your arrow hit glows for 60 seconds, through walls, and takes 20% more damage from you, from any source.

- A hit on another mob moves the mark.
- The marking arrow gets no bonus unless that mob was already marked.
- Never marks players.

`ardanborn:hunt_prey` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Ranger](classes.md#ranger)

### Iron Stomach

Food never gives you Hunger, Poison or Nausea, including raw chicken, pufferfish and suspicious stew. Potions and mobs still can.

`ardanborn:iron_stomach` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Uruk-hai](ancestries.md#uruk-hai)

### Leaf-walker

No fall damage landing on leaves. Speed II and Jump Boost II while on leaves and for 2 seconds after.

- Any leaves, including azalea and mangrove. You can't pass through them.

`ardanborn:leaf_walker` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Silvan](ancestries.md#silvan)

### Lembas

Bread fills hunger and saturation and gives Regeneration I for 5 seconds. 60-second cooldown; until then bread is ordinary.

`ardanborn:lembas` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Silvan](ancestries.md#silvan)

### Light of the Staff

Right-click with a blaze rod: hostile mobs within 6 blocks take 3 hearts of magic damage, are thrown back and glow for 5 seconds. Undead take 6 hearts and burn for 4 seconds. 15-second cooldown.

- Ignores armor, and gets no melee bonuses. Kills count as yours.
- The cooldown shows on your action bar.

`ardanborn:light_of_the_staff` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Loremaster](classes.md#loremaster)

### Mending Craft

The item in your main hand and each worn armor piece regain 1 durability every 10 seconds. Stacks with Mending.

`ardanborn:mending_craft` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Smith](classes.md#smith)

### Moonbound

At night in the Overworld (time 13000-23000): Strength I. Gibbous moon (either): also Speed I. Full moon: Strength II, Speed I and Resistance I.

- The sky needn't be visible.
- Moon phase: `/time query day`, divided by 8; a remainder of 0 is full.

`ardanborn:moonbound` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Beorning](ancestries.md#beorning)

### Pearls of Alqualondë

Luck I while holding a fishing rod in either hand. Each catch has a 25% chance to be doubled. Stacks with Luck of the Sea.

`ardanborn:pearls_of_alqualonde` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Teleri](ancestries.md#teleri)

### Quiet Steps

Speed I while sneaking.

`ardanborn:quiet_steps` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Burglar](classes.md#burglar)

### Sea's Vigor

+2 hearts while in water or rain. They start empty and are lost, with any health in them, when you leave.

`ardanborn:seas_vigor` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Teleri](ancestries.md#teleri)

### Second Breakfast

Food restores 1 more hunger point and 2 more saturation, up to full.

`ardanborn:second_breakfast` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Hobbit](ancestries.md#hobbit)

### Shield Wall

While holding a shield in either hand, you take 15% less damage and other players within 5 blocks take 10% less. Several Defenders don't stack. Not void or `/kill`.

`ardanborn:shield_wall` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Defender](classes.md#defender)

### Silver Tongue

Hero of the Village I within 8 blocks of a villager or wandering trader, and for 3 seconds after.

`ardanborn:silver_tongue` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Minstrel](classes.md#minstrel)

### Smith's Mark

Only you can inscribe [Cirth runes](cirth-runes.md). Twice per full moon, a tool or weapon you craft under open sky gains an enchantment up to 5 levels above its usual maximum.

- **Under a full moon:** craft at night in the Overworld, with nothing at all overhead.
  - Tools and weapons are pickaxes, axes, shovels, hoes, swords, spears, maces, bows, crossbows, tridents and fishing rods. Armor isn't blessed.
  - Every moonlit enchantment starts one level above its vanilla maximum and goes up to **5 levels above it**: Sharpness VI-X, Fortune IV-VIII, Unbreaking IV-VIII, Knockback III-VII, Fire Aspect III-VII. Lower levels are likelier; for Fortune, IV is 5 times as likely as VIII.
  - Quick Charge and Lure stop at V.
  - The roll shows in the result slot before you take the item, and taking the ingredients out and putting them back gives the same roll. Each item you take rolls a new one.
  - **Twice per full moon.** Each moonlit item you take counts, and your action bar shows how many are left. The count is tracked on your character, so logging out or a restart doesn't reset it, and it starts over at the next full moon. After the second, crafting is ordinary until then.
  - Moonlit items must be taken one click at a time; shift-click is refused for them.
  - Check the moon in the sky, or with `/time query day` (full moon when the day divided by 8 leaves 0).
  - Crafter blocks never bless an item.

**How many enchantments**

| Enchantments | Chance |
|---|---|
| 1 | 89% |
| 2 | 10% |
| 3 | 1% |

Each extra one is drawn from what's left, skipping any that clash with what the item already has (Sharpness, Smite and Bane of Arthropods exclude each other, as do Riptide and Loyalty, and Density and Breach).

**Which enchantment**

| Rarity | Weight | Enchantments |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 10 | Efficiency, Sharpness, Power, Quick Charge, Impaling, Density, Lunge, Lure |
| Uncommon | 5 | Unbreaking, Smite, Bane of Arthropods, Knockback, Punch, Piercing, Loyalty, Breach, Luck of the Sea |
| Rare | 2 | Fire Aspect, Sweeping Edge, Riptide, Wind Burst |
| Very rare | 1 | Fortune, Looting |

Never drawn: Silk Touch, Mending, Infinity, Flame, Multishot, Channeling. Only those that fit the item count, so the odds depend on the item. For a diamond pickaxe the choices are Efficiency (10), Unbreaking (5) and Fortune (1): 63% Efficiency, 31% Unbreaking, 6% Fortune.

`ardanborn:smiths_mark` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Smith](classes.md#smith)

### Songs of Power

Sound a goat horn to play its song, within 24 blocks. 90-second cooldown shared by all songs.

- "Allies" below means you and every player within 24 blocks.
- Range is measured from you. Custom horns such as the Horn of Gondor don't play songs.

**Common songs** (horns from goats ramming trees and stone)

| Horn | Song | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Ponder | Song of Rest | Allies: Regeneration I for 12s |
| Sing | Song of Valor | Allies: Speed I for 20s and Regeneration I for 8s |
| Seek | Song of Seeking | Monsters within 32 blocks glow for 10s |
| Feel | The Walking Song | Allies: +3 hunger and Speed I for 30s |

**Greater songs** (horns from screaming goats)

| Horn | Song | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Admire | Song of Courage | Allies: Strength I for 15s and Resistance I for 8s |
| Call | Gathering Call | Allies: Speed II and Jump Boost I for 15s |
| Yearn | Song of Hope | Allies: Poison, Wither, Weakness, Slowness, Mining Fatigue, Hunger, Blindness and Darkness are cleared, then Regeneration I for 5s |
| Dream | Lullaby of Lúthien | Monsters within 10 blocks: Slowness IV for 5s, and they forget who they were chasing |

`ardanborn:songs_of_power` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Minstrel](classes.md#minstrel)

### Spear-hardy

Spears deal 15% more damage, mounted or on foot.

- Adds to [Charge of the Mark](#charge-of-the-mark) and the Rohirrim's items, up to [60%](configuration.md#rohirrim) together.

`ardanborn:spear_hardy` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Rohirrim](ancestries.md#rohirrim)

### Staff of Rest

Two uses, both with a stick named "Staff of Rest" at an anvil (capitals don't matter) in your main hand. The stick isn't used up. Cooldowns are separate and show on your action bar.

- **Right-click a player**, not sneaking: Regeneration II for 8 seconds, cures Poison and Wither, and holds back a [Morgul wound](morgul-wound.md#treatment). 15-second cooldown.
- Holding back restarts the wound's timer; it doesn't cure it. Once every 10 minutes per wounded player, by any Healer.
- **Sneak and right-click**, at anything: you and players within 8 blocks heal 3 hearts, and undead within 8 blocks take 3 hearts of magic damage. Doesn't cure Poison or Wither. 60-second cooldown.
- [Healer of Imladris](#healer-of-imladris) raises the heal to 4.5 hearts for a Peredhil.
- `custom_model_data` for resource packs: `staff_of_rest`. The anvil adds it, as does the first use of a staff that lacks it. Naming the stick anything else takes it off.

`ardanborn:hands_of_the_healer` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Healer](classes.md#healer)

### Star of Eärendil

Regeneration I and Night Vision at night in the Overworld, with open sky overhead. Glass counts as open; leaves don't.

`ardanborn:star_of_earendil` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Peredhil](ancestries.md#peredhil)

### Swift Messenger

Horses, donkeys, mules, camels and llamas you ride have Speed I and take no fall damage.

`ardanborn:swift_messenger` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Peredhil](ancestries.md#peredhil)

### Tide's Fury

Melee and trident hits, thrown included, deal 25% more damage while you're in water or rain. Stacks with the [Spear of the Havens](pearl-of-osse.md#spear-of-the-havens).

`ardanborn:tides_fury` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Teleri](ancestries.md#teleri)

### Tough as Old Tree-roots

30% less damage from the Wither effect, Slowness lasts half as long, and immune to Darkness.

- Slowness from other ArdanBorn or Origins powers isn't shortened.

`ardanborn:tough_as_old_tree_roots` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Hobbit](ancestries.md#hobbit)

### Undying Body

Immune to Poison and Hunger from every source.

`ardanborn:undying_body` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Noldor](ancestries.md#noldor), [Peredhil](ancestries.md#peredhil)

### Unseen

While you sneak, hostile mobs more than 6 blocks away don't notice you.

- A mob you hit can fight back, and one already chasing you may keep chasing.

`ardanborn:unseen` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Burglar](classes.md#burglar)

### Wayfarer

25% chance not to lose a hunger point. Saturation drains as usual.

`ardanborn:wayfarer` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Ranger](classes.md#ranger)

### Woodland Stride

Speed I in woodland biomes and for 3 seconds after leaving.

- Woodland: any biome with *forest*, *taiga*, *jungle*, *grove* or *woods* in its name.

`ardanborn:woodland_stride` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Silvan](ancestries.md#silvan)

### Wrath of the Noldor

Melee hits deal 25% more damage to the [undead](lore-and-items.md#undead) and to creatures of the Nether. Not projectiles.

- Creatures of the Nether: blazes, ghasts, magma cubes, hoglins, piglins, piglin brutes.

`ardanborn:wrath_of_the_noldor` · from ArdanBorn · used by [Noldor](ancestries.md#noldor)
